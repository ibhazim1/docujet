What Is EDMS?

Electronic Document Management System

The Electronic Document Management System (EDMS) is a software system for organising and storing various kinds of documents in multiple formats created throughout an organisation.

The electronic document management software focuses on using and optimising active documents and structured data such as PDF files, Word documents, Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint files.

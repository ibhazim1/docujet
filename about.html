Our Story

Built for trust, governance and operational resilience

Founded in July 2001, Documation is a Malaysia-owned technology company specializing in secure enterprise document and communication management. For over two decades, we have supported financial institutions, insurers, telcos, and regulated enterprises in transforming how mission-critical data is processed, governed, and delivered across physical and digital channels.

Our capabilities span secure automation, intelligent orchestration, and multi-channel delivery, enabling organizations to replace fragmented workflows with controlled, auditable, and highly scalable operations. From raw data ingestion to final customer delivery, we ensure every interaction is executed with precision, security, and accountability.

At Documation, innovation is anchored in trust, governance, and continuous improvement. We operate with a long-term mindset - strengthening operational resilience, supporting regulatory readiness, and enabling our clients to scale with confidence in highly regulated environments.

We do not merely deploy solutions. We build dependable infrastructure that organizations rely on every day.
